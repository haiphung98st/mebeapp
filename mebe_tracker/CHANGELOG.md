# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release of MeBé Tracker.

- Email/Google sign-in, onboarding, and baby profile creation
- Home dashboard with daily summary, quick actions, and recent activity
- Feeding tracker: breastfeeding timer (left/right) and bottle-feeding log
- Pumping tracker: session timer, milk stash management with expiry reminders
- Sleep tracker: live session tracking, history, and smart sleep-window suggestions
- Diaper tracker: quick-log buttons with wet/dirty/both color guide
- Growth tracker: WHO percentile charts, developmental milestones, vaccine schedule
- Push notification reminders for feeding, pumping, sleep, and vaccines
- Premium subscription via in-app purchase (Google Play + App Store) gating AI insights, PDF export, vaccine schedule, and family sharing
- Profile & settings: baby management, family sharing, PDF report export, notification preferences
- Statistics screen with date-range summaries (feeding, sleep, pump, diaper) and local AI insights, plus a weekly report notification
- App-wide Crashlytics error reporting and analytics event logging
